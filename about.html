About CampusFind

CampusFind is a college Lost & Found portal designed to help students report and search for lost and found belongings on campus.

Students can report items, browse existing reports, search by name and category, and contact the person who reported the item.

Technologies Used

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
  • Browser Local Storage
  • GitHub Pages

Project Goal

The goal is to provide a simple digital platform that makes it easier for college students to recover misplaced belongings.
